Credit utilization = reported balance ÷ credit limit × 100. Lower reported utilization is generally favorable, but there is no magic cutoff or guaranteed score change. Reporting dates vary by issuer.

Part 2 · Make Credit Card Decisions

Your statement arrives. Choose how you will repay it. Notice how different decisions affect borrowing costs and payment history.

How will you pay your statement?

Will you make the required payment on time?

Paying in full can generally avoid purchase interest when the card's grace-period conditions are met. Payment timing and credit-bureau reporting timing are not necessarily the same.
