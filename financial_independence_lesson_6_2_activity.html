Part 2 · Vehicle Financing Comparison

Both options finance the same $25,000 vehicle at a 7% annual interest rate. Only the loan term changes. These are standard fully amortizing loans with fixed monthly payments.

Option A · 60 months

Option B · 84 months

The lower payment is not necessarily the cheaper loan. Total loan payments exclude sales tax, registration, insurance, fuel, maintenance, and depreciation.

Part 3 · Grocery Unit-Price Challenge

Compare packages of the same type of rice. A lower unit price can save money, but larger packages require more money upfront and enough storage to use the product before it spoils.

ProductPackage sizePriceUnit price

The simulator assumes you buy whole packages of your selected size and have no existing rice. It compares cash spent to meet at least the selected quantity, not the value of leftovers.

Part 4 · Opportunity Cost Calculator

Use the cost differences you discovered above, or enter your own monthly savings estimate. See how investing that amount could grow over time.

Illustrative future values assume month-end contributions, a fixed annual nominal return divided by 12 for monthly compounding, and no starting balance. Actual returns fluctuate. These projections exclude taxes, fees, and inflation. The loan-payment difference is not pure savings unless total borrowing costs and the longer repayment period are also considered.
