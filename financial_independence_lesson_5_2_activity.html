Model limitations: This is a teaching illustration, not a personal financial forecast. It assumes constant annual returns and salaries growing at a fixed rate. It ignores investment taxes, fees, inflation, employer benefits, unemployment, and the effect of education debt. Savings are calculated from gross earnings, even though real budgets must account for taxes and living expenses. Training costs are deducted in the earnings comparison but are not modeled as investment withdrawals or loans.
