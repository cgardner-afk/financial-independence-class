1. Classify the Transaction Register

Choose a category for each entry. Money received is income; purchases and charges are expenses; money moved between your own accounts is a transfer. A credit card payment is a debt settlement, not a second expense when its underlying purchases are already counted.

Jordan used a credit card to buy groceries and later paid the same amount from checking. The grocery purchase is an expense; the payment is a transfer to settle the card balance. The credit card begins and ends this case with a $0 balance.
