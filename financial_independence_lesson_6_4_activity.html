3 · Financial reality challenges

Real budgets need to adapt. Each challenge is a separate what-if scenario based on your saved initial budget. Changes in one challenge do not carry into the next. Save your initial budget above before beginning.

Challenge 1 · Unexpected repair

A $450 vehicle repair

You have an unexpected $450 repair bill this month. You may use existing money in a vehicle-maintenance sinking fund, if available, and/or reduce this month's other allocations. Your budget must remain balanced.

Using existing reserves covers part of the repair without reducing this month's income. The rest must come from this month's allocations. The reserve is an existing balance, not new income.
