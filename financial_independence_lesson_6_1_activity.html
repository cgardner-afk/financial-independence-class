Financial Independence I · Unit 6 · Lesson 6.1

The Intentional Spending Challenge

Design a spending plan that reflects what you value, respond to real-life choices, and discover how everyday decisions can affect future financial freedom.

Student Information

This activity runs entirely in your browser. It does not automatically save or submit your work to Canvas. Copy or download your results when finished.

1. Choose Your Priorities

You have $600 per month available after essential expenses. Select exactly three categories that matter most to you. These should guide your spending choices.

4. Respond to Spending Challenges

Make a choice in each situation. Some decisions change your monthly plan or long-term savings; others involve one-time tradeoffs. The goal is not to save every dollar, but to make deliberate choices.

Challenge A — The New Phone

Your current phone works, but a new model is available for $35 per month for 24 months. What do you do?

Choose an option to see its tradeoff.

Challenge B — The Subscription Audit

You discover $45 per month in subscriptions you rarely use. How much will you eliminate?

Choose an option to see the annual impact.

Challenge C — The Social Invitation

Friends invite you on a weekend trip costing $250 one time. What do you do?

Choose an option to see the tradeoff.

Challenge D — The Pay Raise

Your take-home pay increases by $200 per month. How much of this raise would you save?
