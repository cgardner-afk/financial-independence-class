How does the calculation work?

The Money Guy methodology assumes an annual rate of 10% for dollars invested at age 20 or younger. For each age above 20, the assumed annual rate declines by 0.1 percentage points, down to 5.5% at age 65. Each investment keeps the rate assigned to the age at which it was made, and growth compounds monthly until age 65.

Formula: Future value = amount × (1 + annual rate ÷ 12)12 × (65 − investment age).

Illustrative projections only, not guaranteed returns. Actual investments fluctuate and can lose value. Figures are nominal and do not adjust for inflation, taxes, or fees. A dollar at age 65 will generally have less purchasing power than a dollar today.
